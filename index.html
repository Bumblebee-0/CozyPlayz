What is Cozy Playz?

Cozy Playz started as a small survival world for a few Tamil friends who just wanted a relaxed place to build, chat, and play without the drama. It's grown into a warm little community — but it's still run the same way: friendly first, competitive second.

Whether you're here to build your dream base, go on nether trips with the group, or just hang out in voice chat talking nonsense — you'll fit right in.
